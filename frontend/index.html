🔐 What is AES?

Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is a symmetric encryption algorithm adopted by the U.S. government. AES-256 uses a 256-bit key, making it virtually unbreakable with current technology.

🛡️ Why Encryption Matters

Encryption protects sensitive information from unauthorized access. It's essential for banking, healthcare, legal communications, and personal privacy in the digital age.

🌍 Real-World Applications

AES is used in: Banking systems (secure transactions), WhatsApp & Signal (end-to-end encryption), HTTPS (secure websites), Government communications, and Data storage encryption.
